# heplacedeternity
A series of manuscripts going over analysis and testimony of the Christian conceptions of the mechanics, margins, menace and majesty of the walk of faith, salvation, perdition and eternal life.

He Placed Eternity is a trilogy investigating the realities of salvation, perdition, and eternal life under the authority of Scripture, amd carefully fused reporting from testimonies ofvalid optasiological events (VOE). The Atlases of Perdition and Eternal Life collect, describe, classify, relate, and assess the particulars of spiritual existence while preserving the distinction between what Scripture establishes, what testimony contributes, and what subsequent reasoning soundly entails.
