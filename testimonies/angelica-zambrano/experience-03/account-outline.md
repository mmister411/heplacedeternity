# AZ-03 — Working account outline (not a transcript)

Based on the [HeavenVisit third-experience account](https://www.heavenvisit.net/angelica-zambrano-3.html). Paraphrased for archive navigation only.

1. **Context and reluctance.** Angelica describes fear and resistance to giving further public testimony, with a report of a new experience during a prayer gathering.
2. **Heavenly vantage point.** She reports being shown churches and their spiritual condition by an angel.
3. **Heavenly garden and children.** The narration presents a garden, children, and angelic guidance.
4. **Descent to the heart of Hell.** The reported route goes toward a named central region via a dark approach and barred gate.
5. **Religious leaders in torment.** The report describes ministers and other people formerly associated with religious service, with punishments interpreted in relation to conduct on Earth.
6. **Further encounters.** The account includes alleged cases involving favoritism, despair, suicide, conflict, and music, accompanied by exhortations to listeners. Those moral assertions are claims of the source.
7. **Persistent physical and psychological suffering.** The account describes fire, restraints, tormenting beings, memory, inability to leave, and remembered vocations.
8. **Return to testimony.** The narration emphasizes a duty to speak about the reported experience.

**Preliminary research retrieval keys (not Atlas phenomena):** heart/center of Hell; descent; bars and gates; routes; demons; flames; cells or enclosures; restraints; serpents; sermons performed as torment; reciprocal conduct; refusal of release; heavenly children; angelic instructions.

**Do not** treat a claimed fate of any living or deceased individual as verified, nor the report's claims about suicide or other conduct as settled doctrine. These are catalogued as attributed speech within a testimony.
