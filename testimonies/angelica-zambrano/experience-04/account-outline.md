# AZ-04 — Working account outline (not a transcript)

Based on the [HeavenVisit fourth-experience text](https://www.heavenvisit.net/angelica-zambrano-4.html). The source itself says this is a **partial** telling.

1. **Setting.** Angelica reports prayer with another Christian woman and a new experience in which she perceives separation from her body.
2. **State of the church.** She describes vessels or jars containing oil, with differing conditions used to symbolize the spiritual condition of Christian congregants.
3. **Visit to Hell.** The narrative moves to a region in which demons allegedly assemble and organize tasks.
4. **Book of names.** She reports a large book associated with demons and lists of Christian ministers or workers targeted for destruction.
5. **Prison-like places.** The account describes numbered jail-like enclosures and demons receiving punishment from other demons.
6. **Instruction to testify.** The narrative stresses preaching, disclosure of visions, spiritual vigilance, and responsibility toward others.
7. **Mercy and return.** The witness recounts a message about divine love and recalls aspects of her previous experiences.

**Preliminary retrieval keys (not Atlas phenomena):** oil jars; symbolic representations of church members; infernal stairways or raised positions; book/list of names; numbered enclosures; demonic planning; demons as prisoners; assigned tormenting agents; spiritual warfare rhetoric.

**Note:** The reported material is catalogued as testimony. It does not establish the existence or accuracy of the purported infernal book, the fate of any specific person, or the reported spiritual operations. Atlas extraction and inclusion require the author's separate approval.
