# The Atlas of Eternal Life

The Atlas of Eternal Life is a member of the He Place Eternity collection. It examines what Scripture holds concerning what awaits those whom the Lord knows, and what a modern-day, God-enabled Isaiah, Patmos, or Lazarus might have seen regarding the mechanics, margins, and majesty of the blessed side of stepping into eternity.

It uses the [shared testimony collection](../testimonies/), [POE register](../poe_register.tsv), and [research apparatus](../apparatus/). Source documents are held once, even when a reported experience includes both Paradise and Perdition. The existing apparatus records are chiefly Perdition-focused and retain that scope until Eternal Life observations are added.
