# The Atlas of Perdition

The Atlas of Perdition is a member of the He Place Eternity collection. It examines what Scripture holds concerning perdition and what a modern-day, God-enabled Daniel, Job, or Lazarus might have seen regarding the mechanics, margins, and menace of being cast into perdition.

## Testimony Collection

To be operated on in the apparatus, the testimony must first be collected and then authorized for inclusion. Testimonies are collected, often with multiple formats (pdf, docx, markdown, html extract, etc) from different source locations (webpage, blog, discussion board, streaming extract) but usually not kindle ripping or epub extraction. This form of material is usually intended to be shared and HPE will not be reproducing large swaths of testimonies in it's evetual manuscripts. Inclusion of a testimony of a potential VOE is no judgement of confirmed HPE perspective as a VOE.  Some optasiological reports may be found to be invalid by way of confusion or grift, or be of incompatible spiritual orgin, as there are nefarious forces out there in creation.
