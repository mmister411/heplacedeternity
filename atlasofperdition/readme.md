# The Atlas of Perdition

The Atlas of Perdition is a member of the He Place Eternity collection. It examines what Scripture holds concerning perdition and what a modern-day, God-enabled Daniel, Job, or Lazarus might have seen regarding the mechanics, margins, and menace of being cast into perdition.

## Research apparatus

The working apparatus catalogues source-anchored phenomena across VOE testimony from units that have been authorized for inclusion by the author of He Placed Eternity (HPE). It preserves distinctions between witnessed observations such as but not limited to, sensations, heard speech, claimed knowledge, attributed speech, and interpretation. Its second-order taxonomy contains subclasses within primary divisions.

- [Corpus Survey](apparatus/Atlas_of_Perdition_Corpus_Survey.md) — scope and composition of the collected accounts.
- [Scene Inventory](apparatus/Atlas_of_Perdition_Scene_Inventory.md) — scenes and testimony-unit boundaries.
- [Phenomena Register](apparatus/Atlas_of_Perdition_Phenomena_Register.md) — source-anchored descriptions of the phenomena.
- [Cross-Witness Concordance](apparatus/Atlas_of_Perdition_Cross_Witness_Concordance.md) — recurring motifs and important differences among accounts.
- [Taxonomic Architecture](apparatus/Atlas_of_Perdition_Taxonomic_Architecture.md) — definitions and boundaries for the second-order classes.

The [apparatus directory](apparatus/) also contains structured datasets and the scripts that generated the indexes. The underlying collected testimony text is maintained separately and is not included in this public repository.

## Testimony Collection

To be operated on in the apparatus, the testimony must first be collected and then authorized for inclusion. Testimonies are collected, often with multiple formats (pdf, docx, markdown, html extract, etc) from different source locations (webpage, blog, discussion board, streaming extract) but usually not kindle ripping or epub extraction. This form of material is usually intended to be shared and HPE will not be reproducing large swaths of testimonies in it's evetual manuscripts. Inclusion of a testimony of a potential VOE is no judgement of confirmed HPE perspective as a VOE.  Some optasiological reports may be found to be invalid by way of confusion or grift, or be of incompatible spiritual orgin, as there are nefarious forces out there in creation.
