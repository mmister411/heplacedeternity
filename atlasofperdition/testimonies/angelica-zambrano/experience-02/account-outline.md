# AZ-02 — Working account outline (not a transcript)

Paraphrased from the [HeavenVisit second-experience text](https://www.heavenvisit.net/angelica-zambrano-2.html). Research navigation only; no Atlas admission.

1. **Second departure:** Angelica recounts a second apparent out-of-body event in the presence of people attending her at home, and an ascent escorted by angels.
2. **Heavenly setting:** She reports a garden and renewed encounters with Jesus and angels.
3. **Books and rewards:** The narrative describes heavenly registers or books, crowns, and rewards.
4. **Scenes of judgment:** Angelica reports a further visit to Hell, with accounts of people associated with particular forms of religious or moral failure, including misuse of money and idolatry.
5. **Individuals and symbols:** The account includes clergy, children, named figures, and material markers of condemnation. All these are claims attributed to the narrative, not established findings.
6. **Return and exhortation:** The source ends with urgent directions concerning religious conduct and preparation for Christ's return.

**Preliminary retrieval keys (not Atlas phenomena):** heavenly book/register; crowns; angels; distinct infernal sections; imprisoned souls; fire; religious office; labeled condemnation; spiritual memory and regret.
