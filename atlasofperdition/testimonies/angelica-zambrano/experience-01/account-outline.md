# AZ-01 — Working account outline (not a transcript)

**Source followed:** the [edited English text hosted by Encinar de Mamré](https://www.encinardemamre.com/Angelica-Zambrano-testimony-in-English.html). This outline paraphrases the report and preserves the sequence of its sections. It is an aid to navigating the testimony collection, not verification of events and not Atlas material.

## Narrative sequence

1. **Context and preparation.** Angelica describes an earlier conversion, later religious struggles, fasting, prayer, and anticipation of a revelation of Heaven and Hell. Her mother Maxima gives a parallel account of the family's preparation.
2. **Reported departure from the body.** The mother describes an apparent loss of breath and other outward signs during prayer; Angelica recounts a perceived separation from her body and the appearance of Jesus and angels. These descriptions remain the witnesses' reported perceptions, not independently established medical facts.
3. **Passage toward Hell.** The account describes a rapid descent through a dark passage, increasing heat, terror, and a nauseating atmosphere.
4. **Infernal landscape and enclosures.** Angelica reports cells, tormenting creatures, fire, worms, and individuals asking for release. The account portrays recognition, memory, and regret as components of suffering.
5. **Differentiated punishments and claimed encounters.** Reported persons are associated with particular conduct during earthly life, including hypocrisy, lack of forgiveness, and other offenses. The narrative includes allegations about specific deceased public figures; those allegations are not independently verified.
6. **Demonic activity.** Angelica describes beings who harass prisoners, the organization of torment, and a gathering where demons allegedly plan activities directed toward people on Earth.
7. **Approach of others toward Hell.** A separate passage presents people moving or falling toward Hell, including professing believers. The narrative presents warning and opportunity to repent as central themes.
8. **Children and images.** The report includes children in Hell and assigns moral significance to entertainment, family conduct, and religious formation. These are claims of the source, not independent conclusions.
9. **Transition to Heaven.** The account shifts from Hell to the Kingdom of Heaven and depicts a passage out of the infernal region.
10. **Heavenly environment.** Angelica reports a radiant setting, praise, heavenly architecture, and scenes involving angels, Jesus, and redeemed people.
11. **Return and future warning.** The source describes a vision of events associated with Christ's return, then an instruction that Angelica return to life and report her experience.
12. **Family account and aftermath.** Maxima describes Angelica's physical condition after the reported return and the delay before she could tell the experience fully. The published presentation ends with exhortations, prayer, and further narration.

## Features to investigate later

These are **research search keys**, not validated Atlas phenomena:

- **Topography:** descent, tunnel, thresholds, cells, routes, distinct infernal and heavenly areas.
- **Atmosphere and senses:** darkness, heat, odors, terror, amplified distress, sound.
- **Beings:** angels, demonic tormentors, serpentine imagery, human souls.
- **Punishment patterns:** fire, worms, confinement, bodily harm, remembered actions, alleged repetition or correspondence to earthly conduct.
- **Speech and interpretation:** witness speech versus speech attributed to Jesus, angels, tormented persons, or an editor.
- **Transmission:** mother's statements, testimony's principal narration, documentary framing, translation choices, later abridgment.
