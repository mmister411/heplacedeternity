## Boundaries

The published narrative includes speech attributed to **Angelica**, her mother **Maxima**, and narration/afterword material. All are part of an edited presentation, but the statements of each speaker must remain distinguishable. The named people allegedly seen in Hell are claims *within* this testimony; the collection does not adopt those allegations as established facts.

Other translations, mirrors, video copies, or later interviews about the **same first experience** should be indexed here as separate **accounts/versions**. A separately reported second, third, or fourth experience belongs in its own numbered experience folder, if source boundaries confirm it.
