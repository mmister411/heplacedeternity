# Testimony Source Discovery — First Pass

**Project:** Atlas of Perdition  
**Date:** 16 November 2026  
**Scope:** The 59-entry names list supplied by the collector, beginning with Earthquake Kelley and ending with Yong Thang.

This directory contains a preliminary source-discovery register, not newly ingested Atlas phenomena and not a set of redistributed testimonies. Source titles and linked pages are leads for further examination. A web search match does not independently establish a witness's identity, the authority or completeness of a transcription, a reported experience, or permission to publish a copy.

## What is committed

The file `source_discovery_register.tsv` records the exact name as listed, provisional discovery status, source-type hints, and up to two URL leads per name. These links are citations or access routes, not repository-hosted copies of the linked works.

**No third-party testimony text has been copied into this public repository.** Availability online is not itself a redistribution license. The right to republish each complete text or transcript remains to be established, or an authorized copy must be supplied.

## Before promoting a lead into the Atlas

1. Open the linked source and identify the actual speaker, title, format, and account boundaries.
2. Determine whether it contains an account of Perdition; preserve heavenly or non-infernal material separately when relevant to the parent project.
3. Preserve a permitted source artifact and record the origin, its language or version when known, and its degree of mediation (direct testimony, transcript, translation, summary, interview).
4. Segment narrative units and extract source-anchored claims with the established knowledge modes.
5. Assign stable identifiers without renumbering existing Atlas records; classify new phenomena and review cross-witness motifs.
6. Validate indexes and document unresolved identity, provenance, or textual questions.
