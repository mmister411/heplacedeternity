## Edition distinctions

- The Bryan Melvin excerpt follows [Randy Kay and Shaun Tabatt's Destiny Image publication](https://www.destinyimage.com/blog/randy-kay-shaun-tabatt-he-died-saw-hitler-in-hell); it should not be assigned to *In a Land Unknown* on the compilation alone.
- The Jennifer Perez compilation contains her self-introduction, retained in the [Insights of God PDF](https://www.insightsofgod.com/downloads/english_jennifer_perez.pdf) but omitted from the alternative DivineRevelations PDF.
- Carmelo Brenes's wording agrees with the [English PDF](https://www.insightsofgod.com/downloads/carmelo_brenes_englishpdf.pdf); the website's HTML transcription has variant introductory phrasing.
- Daniel Ekechukwu's `DAN` and `DAN-DA2` are different tellings of the same reported experience: one follows the Kent/Fotherby book text and the other the CBN account.
- Bernarda Fernández's `FER` text contains two journeys. The [FreeDVDTracts PDF](https://www.freedvdtracts.com/documents/Bernadda_Fernandez_Prepare_for_the_Lords_Return.pdf) matches the first-journey lead-in more closely than the other linked PDF.

Edition matching remains **passage-level**; variations beyond the checked passages are unresolved.
