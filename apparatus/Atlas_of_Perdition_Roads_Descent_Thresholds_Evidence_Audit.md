# Atlas of Perdition — Roads, Descent, and Thresholds: Evidence Audit 03

**Working edition:** 16 November 2026  
**Status:** Close-reading audit of four route-and-threshold motif families.  
**Primary source:** Pasted text(20261116-025409).txt, with line numbers counted from the supplied compilation.  
**Scope:** Descent and falling; rotating gateways; gates, doors, and ruptured boundaries; and roads, paths, and branching movement.

## Method

A **route** is a narrated course of movement. A **threshold** is a boundary that can open, be crossed, or remain closed. An **arrival** is an endpoint and does not, by itself, describe a route. A **procession** is movement by persons and may take place inside a tunnel rather than on a road. These distinctions determine motif membership.

The witnesses' observations (O), felt experiences (F), heard or attributed speech (H/S), and interpretations (I) are not treated as interchangeable. Source parallels do not establish shared geography, independent origin, or doctrinal confirmation. The four Angelica Zambrano experiences remain separate testimony units attributed to one named witness.

**Findings.** Travel through a passage, abrupt vertical falling, and a felt falling sensation form distinct modes. A narrated underground location does not prove a uniform depth. **Mapping correction.** AZ3-011 (line 3146) describes walking a dark alley to an opening barred gate, with no descent. WIE-001 (line 3891) says the witness was suddenly placed or dropped into a prison cell, without a perceived fall or route. They have been removed from this descent family. AZ3-011 remains in the gate motif; WIE-001 remains in the cells motif.

## II. Vortices and spinning funnels — two testimony units

| Unit | Source and records | Observed transit and delivery |
|---|---|---|

| **Baxter** | Lines 423, 427; BAX-001, BAX-002 | Observes multiple large funnels projecting from earth and turning inward and back; then enters one and experiences it as a spinning tunnel. |

The unit of agreement is **rotational passage or delivery**. Baxter's earth-protruding gateways and Melvin's cell-delivery vortices are different operations. The two descriptions from each narrator concern that narrator's one testimony unit; four register records do not constitute four independent witnesses.

This motif requires no membership change.

The **direction and function of a threshold differ**: entry from outside, internal passage, demonic outward egress, an un-crossed door, or a boundary opened by a nonstandard act. A door in the narrative should not automatically be counted as the main gate to all of Hell. For BAX-097, the physical door is reported as seen, while the bottomless-pit destination is attributed to guide speech at line 1305 (see Audit 02).

This motif requires no membership change.

## IV. Wide roads and branching paths — five testimony units

| Unit | Source and records | Road or path form |
|---|---|---|
| **Brenes** | Line 3574; BRE-019 | A wide street carries people apparently engaged in worship straight toward Hell, while a path identified as the Lord's road turns right. The guide supplies a moral explanation. |
| **Melvin** | Lines 2025–2026; MEL-011 | A dusty road passes regions of cells; demonic escorts lead newly arrived people to their assigned enclosures. No branching road is reported there. |
| **Baxter** | Line 503; BAX-019 | A winding path goes among recurrent pits. Its width and any moral fork are not stated. |
| **Perez** | Line 2087; PER-003 | The witness finds herself on a broad road after falling; its destination is unknown to her. |
| **Athet Pyan** | Lines 3733–3735; APY-008, APY-009, APY-013 | A wide left branch and narrow right branch divide at a signed fork. The witness walks the wide road and cannot catch two people ahead. |

The reports support at least three forms: **a morally interpreted fork**, **a logistical delivery road**, and **an unbranched connecting path**. Athet Pyan's sign is part of that testimony, not an inscription observed in Brenes's scene. The resemblance between broad/narrow roads and Matthew 7:13–14 is a textual and thematic correspondence; the narratives need not refer to one road network.

**Mapping correction.** AZ1-024 (line 2616) describes chained persons walking **inside a tunnel**, not along an observed road. FER-013 (line 3828) describes chained people traveling through a tunnel and approaching Hell, not a road. Both have been removed from this road family and remain in **Chains and physical binding**.

## Scriptural correspondence boundary

**Matthew 7:13–14** supplies the broad and narrow way imagery, but it does not establish that a witness's visible fork is a verified underworld road. **Matthew 16:18** uses the language of the gates of Hades but does not specify the materials or mechanism of a gate in these accounts. Biblical movement through depths and passages should be checked in context before being used to equate the testimonies' geography. In the existing register, analogy and related principle remain separate from direct scriptural description.
